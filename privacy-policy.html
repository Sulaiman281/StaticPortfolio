Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to WitShells ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, use our mobile applications (including WheelToWin), or engage with our services, including game development, backend programming, mentorship, blockchain development, and AI automation services.

1.1 About WheelToWin

WheelToWin is a mobile car racing game developed and published by WitShells. The game features competitive racing events where players can earn in-game currency and participate in various racing challenges. WheelToWin is available on Google Play Store and represents one of our flagship game development projects showcasing our expertise in Unity 3D game development and backend systems.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Mobile Game Data (WheelToWin)

When you play WheelToWin, we may collect:

  • Device information (device model, operating system version, device identifiers)
  • Game progress and statistics (race times, achievements, levels completed)
  • In-game purchases and virtual currency transactions
  • Gameplay analytics and user interaction data
  • Crash reports and technical performance data
  • Player preferences and settings

2.2 Personal Information

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:

  • Contact us through our website contact forms
  • Communicate with us via WhatsApp, email, or social media
  • Subscribe to our services or newsletters
  • Participate in our mentorship programs
  • Use our payment services for coffee donations or project payments

This information may include:

  • Name and contact information
  • Email address and phone number
  • Project requirements and specifications
  • Payment information (processed securely through third-party providers)
  • Communication preferences

2.3 Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:

  • IP address and device information
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time spent on our site
  • Referring website information
  • Usage patterns and preferences

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and deliver our services (game development, programming, mentorship)
  • Operate and improve WheelToWin: Maintain game servers, process in-game transactions, track progress, and enhance gameplay experience
  • Game Analytics: Analyze player behavior to improve game balance, fix bugs, and develop new features
  • Communicate with you about projects, services, and game updates
  • Process payments and transactions (including in-app purchases)
  • Provide customer support for WheelToWin and other services
  • Improve our website, games, and services
  • Send you updates about our work and new offerings
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Protect against fraud and unauthorized access

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With trusted third-party providers who assist us in delivering our services (e.g., payment processors, hosting providers, analytics services for WheelToWin)
  • Game Services: With platform providers (Google Play Store) as required for game distribution and in-app purchases
  • Analytics Partners: With analytics providers to understand game usage and improve WheelToWin performance
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights and safety
  • Business Transfers: In connection with any merger, sale, or acquisition of our business
  • Consent: When you have given us explicit consent to share your information

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience and analyze website traffic. We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our site. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences.

7. Third-Party Services

Our website and mobile applications may contain links to or integrate with third-party services, including:

  • Google Play Services: For WheelToWin distribution, in-app purchases, and analytics
  • Unity Analytics: For game performance monitoring and player behavior analysis
  • Social media platforms (LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram)
  • Payment processors (Wise, cryptocurrency wallets, Google Play Billing)
  • Communication platforms (WhatsApp)
  • Portfolio and project hosting services
  • Analytics services (Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics)

These third-party services have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices.

8. In-App Purchases and Virtual Currency

WheelToWin may offer in-app purchases for virtual currency and game enhancements. All transactions are processed through Google Play Store's secure payment system. We do not store your payment information directly. Virtual currency and in-game items have no real-world value and cannot be exchanged for real money.

9. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access to your personal information
  • Correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion of your personal information
  • Restriction of processing
  • Data portability
  • Objection to processing

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information provided below.

11. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected such information, we will take steps to delete it.

12. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure that such transfers comply with applicable data protection laws and implement appropriate safeguards.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

14. WheelToWin Game Information

14.1 Game Verification

WheelToWin is an official product of WitShells, developed by our team led by Syed Suleman Shah (Unity Game Developer) and Naima Ghulam Muhammad (Backend Developer). This game represents our expertise in:

  • Unity 3D game development and optimization
  • Real-time multiplayer racing mechanics
  • Backend server architecture and AWS hosting
  • In-app purchase integration and virtual economy
  • Cross-platform mobile game deployment

14.2 Game Features and Data Processing

WheelToWin includes the following features that may process your data:

  • Racing Events: Competitive racing tournaments with leaderboards
  • Virtual Currency System: Earn and spend in-game money for car upgrades
  • Progress Tracking: Save game progress, achievements, and statistics
  • Performance Analytics: Monitor game performance and user experience
  • Social Features: Compare scores and compete with other players

14.3 Technical Implementation

Our team at WitShells has implemented WheelToWin using industry-standard technologies and security practices. The game is hosted on AWS infrastructure and follows Google Play Store guidelines for data privacy and security.
