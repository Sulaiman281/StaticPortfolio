🌟 What is WitCoin?

WitCoin is a digital coin designed to reward users for their engagement within the WitShells ecosystem. Earn coins by spending time, learning, and participating in activities. WitCoin allows you to unlock rewards, grow your balance, and enjoy gamified learning experiences—with secure, reliable account management including password reset and account deletion options.

💰 How to Earn Digital Coins

⛏️ Cloud Mining

Start cloud mining by tapping the "Mine" button. Coins accumulate automatically over time, making earning effortless.

🧠 Daily Quiz Challenges

Answer 10 daily quizzes correctly to earn coins. You can also create quizzes and invite friends—both of you earn rewards!

🏆 Prize Bound System

Participate in the Prize Bound System for a chance to receive exclusive rewards. Coins you earn help you unlock bounds and maximize your prizes.

⚙️ How the Ecosystem Works

  • Fair Distribution: Rewards are allocated transparently based on engagement.
  • Anti-Abuse Measures: The system ensures fair play across users.
  • Growing Value: The more you engage, the more valuable your coins and ecosystem become.

🌍 The Vision Behind WitCoin

WitCoin aims to create a global reward ecosystem where learning, gaming, and engagement are recognized and rewarded. Every minute spent contributes to building your coin balance and unlocking experiences within WitShells.
