⚡ What is WitShells?

WitShells is a sponsor-funded competitive gaming platform. Players mine WitCoin — a free virtual in-game currency (not a cryptocurrency) — every day at no cost. That WitCoin becomes the entry stake for head-to-head skill matches: chess, checkers, 3D racing, and more.

Sponsors deposit real money into a prize pool. When a sponsor fund is active, every match winner earns real cash paid directly from that fund — zero platform markup, 100% to the player. No luck mechanics. You win by outplaying your opponent.

⚠ WitCoin is a virtual in-game currency only — not a blockchain token, not an investment.

💰 How You Earn Real Money

⛏️ 1 — Mine WitCoin Daily — Free

Open the app, tap Mine, and passively earn ~5–6 WitCoin per day. Keep a daily streak to boost your rate. Zero investment. Zero hardware. Always free.

♞ 2 — Stake in a Skill Match

Use your mined WitCoin as the entry fee for a head-to-head skill game — chess, checkers, and more. Each match requires real skill. No luck involved.

🏆 3 — Win and Earn Real $

Beat your opponent when a sponsor fund is active and earn real cash credited directly to your WitShells wallet. Withdraw to USDT or fiat when ready.

🔄 Win or Lose — What Happens

💵 Sponsor Fund Active

Winner receives real $ from the sponsor pool + a portion of WitCoin stake returned.
Loser's match stake is burned — this shrinks total WitCoin supply, increasing scarcity and value of coins held.

⏳ No Active Sponsor Fund

Winner receives the loser's full WitCoin stake — grow your balance and stay prepared.
No real money payout in this mode. Keep mining, keep winning, stay ready for when funds go live.

🏛 WitCoin Prize Bonds

Convert WitCoin into prize bond tickets and enter community draws funded by app revenue. Changed your mind? Sell the bond back for the exact WitCoin you paid — zero downside, real upside.
