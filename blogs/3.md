# Journey of Syed Suleman Shah  
*Life In Tech Industry*

---

## Beginnings (2016–2017)

My journey began in 2016, when I developed an interest in creating GTA San Andreas servers. By 2017, I had built my own mini-mission server and started learning Unity Game Development, all while studying for my intermediate exams.

---

## Academic Focus (2018)

2018 was a year dedicated to academics, focusing on ISSB and university entry test preparation.

---

## A New Chapter: University Life (2019)

In 2019, a new journey began as I enrolled as a Software Engineering student at the University of Sindh, Jamshoro.  
I participated in coding challenges and exhibitions at Mehran and QUEST universities, and developed my own console-based C++ typing games. Although I was already skilled in Unity 3D, I chose to learn programming from the very core to build a deeper understanding.

For the first time, I experienced developing a graphical user interface desktop app. At the end of 2019, a friend suggested JavaFX—a GUI framework for Java. I spent half a year learning it, and followed Processing GUI tutorials by "Coding Train" to develop a solid understanding of simulations.

---

## Freelancing & Growth (2020–2021)

By the end of 2020, I was confident enough to develop any kind of desktop application. With the mercy of Almighty Allah, I received my first order: a Smart House simulation in JavaFX. I earned $600 within a two-month period, thanks to TeacherOn. From Where I received it.
From here, my freelance journey started. I became independent and began paying my own expenses—an amazing feeling.

In 2021, I started getting various tasks related to Java and JavaFX on Fiverr, earning 40+ positive feedbacks while maintaining a 5-star rating. In August 2021, I returned to Unity 3D and slowly transformed myself as a developer.

---

## Unity, VR, and Final Year Projects (2022)

By 2022, I utilized my Fiverr profile to get Unity 3D-related jobs. As it was my final year, I also started working on my FYP project: "Chor or Police" multiplayer game. I completed 5–6 Unity game projects, learned multiplayer development with Mirror and NetCode, and picked up many new tactics.  
I also began learning VR development with my newly acquired Quest 2.

---

## Graduation, VR Success, and New Challenges (2023–2024)

In 2023, I graduated as a Software Engineer with first position. I completed my first VR project, which gave me a wonderful start to the year. VR projects kept coming—I averaged two projects each month, with revenue ranging from $800–$1500 until October.  
I deleted my Fiverr account after witnessing the Fiverr CEO supporting genocide in Gaza. Afterwards, I survived 2024 with returning buyers and found a few new clients through hard searching.

This did not stop me. I had little experience with REST APIs, so I dedicated myself to learning core development in REST APIs and microservices using ASP.NET Core and Spring Boot. I completed two production-ready REST APIs for a client, which are currently hosted on mobile stores. (playstore, applestore) 
I also developed a chess game with a unique feature: selecting a custom army. I built its three microservices for AI, multiplayer, and game record. 2024 was a really productive year. I started teaching onsite at NexSkill. After applying to several companies, I got contract-based projects from Mixeal and Expetide.

---

## New Beginnings (2025)

In 2025, I am now married to my life and code partner. After marriage, I am looking for a full-time opportunity to utilize my skills for a stable income. In the meantime, I am building reusable assets for game development.
